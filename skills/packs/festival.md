  This is a festival or a party: a big, busy crowd. Safety comes first: anything unsafe goes to the person on duty at once. Food questions go by the menu and the stall times; lost things go to lost and found.
tasks:
  - Make the volunteer shift plan (gates, stalls, cleanup)
  - Confirm the food stalls, the menu and dietary options
  - Name the person on duty for safety at every hour, with a first aid kit
  - Set up a lost and found spot and tell the volunteers where it is
---
The desks a festival or party runs on: a volunteer desk for shifts, a food desk that counts what is served, a safety desk where every incident reaches the person on duty, and lost and found.
