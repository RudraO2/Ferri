desks: [help, judging, mentors, volunteers, checkin]
instructions: |
  This is a hackathon. Participants build in teams against the clock; mentors help teams think, judges score demos. Point technical problems to the help desk, mentor requests to the mentors desk, and keep scores and judging talk out of the participants' spaces.
tasks:
  - Write the judging criteria and the scoring scale
  - Recruit mentors and set their hours
  - Make the volunteer shift plan (registration, food, the help desk)
  - Import the guest list for check-in
  - Print or share the wifi details and the submission link
---
The desks a hackathon runs on: a help desk where the Builder helps with technical problems at any time, a private judging desk with a live score sheet, a mentors desk where teams ask for help, a volunteer desk for shifts, and a check-in desk that ticks guests off the guest list.
