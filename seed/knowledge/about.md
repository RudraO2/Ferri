# About Lantern Labs

Lantern Labs is a volunteer-run builder community in Bengaluru. Anyone who builds things is welcome, whatever their level.

## Contact the organizers
Email hello@lanternlabs.example or post in the community group. Organizers usually reply within a day.

## Joining the community
Joining is free. Members get early invites to every event. There is no membership fee.
