## Code of conduct
Be kind and respectful. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated, online or at the venue. Organizers may remove anyone who breaks the code of conduct, without a refund.

## Reporting a problem
Tell any organizer wearing an orange Lantern Labs badge, or email safety@lanternlabs.example. Reports are handled privately.

## Photos
Photos are taken at events. If you do not want to be photographed, pick up a red lanyard at the registration desk.
