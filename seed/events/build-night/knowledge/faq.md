## Who can join
Anyone 18 or older can join, students and working developers alike. You do not need to know how to code to join a team; designers and product people are welcome.

## Registration
Registration is free and closes on 18 November 2026 at 11:59 PM IST. You must bring a government photo ID that matches your registration name.

## Teams
Teams have 2 to 4 people. You can come solo; team formation happens at 10:30 AM on Saturday.

## Food
Breakfast is served at 9:30 AM, lunch at 1:30 PM and dinner at 8:30 PM on Saturday, and a midnight snack at 12:30 AM. Sunday breakfast is at 8:00 AM. All meals are vegetarian, with vegan and Jain options on request at registration.

## Wi-Fi
The Wi-Fi network is LanternGuest and the password is buildnight2026.

## What to bring
Bring your laptop, charger, a government photo ID, and a refillable water bottle. Sleeping bags are allowed in the quiet room.

## Prizes
First place wins ₹40,000, second place ₹25,000, third place ₹10,000. Every team that demos gets swag.

## Judging
Judging starts at 8:00 AM on Sunday. Each team gets 4 minutes to demo and 2 minutes of questions. Projects must be started at the event; you may use open-source libraries.
